Adversarial Analyst

The bull case is credible, but the capital burden arrives before all benefits: first-half capex exceeded operating cash flow by more than $1B. Large-load execution therefore increases financing, construction and regulatory exposure. The current 19.6× multiple already anticipates some of the data-center upside, while Value Line's long-term base EPS growth is only 6.5%. Dividend growth is expected to slow to about 3%, reducing the income component's ability to rescue a high entry multiple. The strongest rebuttal is the contracted nature of the data-center pipeline; that evidence prevents a SELL verdict, but it does not make the current strike a base-case 10% return.
